Our presence

A coordination network across six Indian cities, headquartered in Mumbai.

Our Indian office network is intended to support business coordination, sourcing communication and market connectivity. Specific office addresses, operating details and contact information should be added only after verification.

Why the address fields are empty. Publishing an unverified address is worse than publishing none: it fails vendor registration checks and it wastes a visitor's trip. Each card below shows only what has been confirmed. As details are verified they are added to data/markets.js and appear here automatically.

How the network is used

The cities cover India's main industrial and trading corridors. In practice that means supplier visits, sample collection and consolidation can be arranged near the source rather than routed through one location.

Mumbai handles headquarters functions and international coordination. The other cities support regional supplier contact and order follow-up.

All enquiries, wherever they originate, reach the same team through the main company email.
