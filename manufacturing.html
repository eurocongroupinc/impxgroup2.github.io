Manufacturing

Product development and manufacturing coordination with partner units, driven by your specification and verified per product.

How manufacturing coordination works

IMPX coordinates production with partner manufacturing units rather than presenting their capacity as its own. What that means in practice: your specification is reviewed, matched to a unit that can genuinely make it, and the production run is coordinated and followed up from here.

It also means that capability questions get a specific answer per product, rather than a general claim. If a partner cannot hold your tolerance or issue the certificate you need, that is said before an order is taken.

Manufacturing capabilities, facilities and production details are subject to product-specific verification. No factory ownership, production capacity, machinery list or certification is claimed on this website.

What the coordination covers

Product development

Turning a concept, sample or competitor reference into a specification a manufacturer can quote against.

Partner coordination

Identifying units whose process and equipment actually suit the product, then managing the relationship through the run.

Requirement analysis

Volume, tolerance, finish and cost target assessed together, because changing one of them usually changes the others.

Technical specification review

Drawings and datasheets checked for gaps before they reach the factory, where gaps become assumptions.

Quality documentation review

Agreeing which test reports and certificates the unit can issue, and what they will and will not cover.

Packaging and dispatch

Packing specified for the transport mode and the destination climate, then coordinated with the shipment.
