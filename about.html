About IMPX Global Traders

A Mumbai-based business built around three things: sourcing what buyers actually specified, coordinating manufacturing with partner units, and being straight about what is not yet confirmed.

Who we are

IMPX GLOBAL TRADERS PRIVATE LIMITED is a Mumbai-based business focused on manufacturing, trading and third-party sourcing across industrial goods, electronics and agricultural products. The company connects buyers and suppliers through structured sourcing, product coordination and B2B trade support.

Most of the work sits between a buyer who knows their specification and a supplier who can meet it. Finding that match, confirming it in writing and moving the goods with the right documentation is the whole job.

Year established: 2011. This date was provided by the company and has not been independently verified on this website. Confirm it against the incorporation documents before presenting it as a verified fact in tenders or vendor registrations.

Mission

To connect businesses with dependable product sourcing, manufacturing coordination and international trading opportunities through professional communication and long-term relationships.

Vision

To develop a trusted and internationally connected business platform for industrial, electronic and agricultural product supply.

Core values

Written as working rules rather than slogans, because these are the ones that decide what gets quoted and what gets declined.

Integrity and transparency

Unverified details are labelled as unverified, including on this website.

Customer-focused communication

One point of contact, and specifications restated back to you before quotation.

Responsible sourcing

Supplier documentation is reviewed before an offer, not after a rejection.

Supplier coordination

Multi-line and mixed-category orders consolidated rather than split across vendors.

Continuous improvement

Every failed shipment or rejected lot changes how the next enquiry is screened.

International collaboration

Destination rules drive the offer, rather than being discovered at the port.
