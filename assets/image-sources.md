## Rules for any image added later

- Check the provider's current licence terms before commercial publication. Terms change,
  and a licence that was free for personal use last year may not cover commercial use now.
- Do not assume an image containing a recognisable person, brand, logo or protected
  property is cleared for all uses. It usually is not.
- Never scrape images from competitors' websites or from search results.
- Record the source URL, licence name, attribution requirement and date obtained above.
- Prefer photographs of goods you have actually supplied. They outperform stock images
  on a B2B site, and they carry no licensing risk at all.

## Technical requirements for replacements

- 4:3 aspect ratio for product images, 16:10 for category cards
- At least 1200px wide, WebP or optimised JPEG, ideally under 200 KB
- Neutral or relevant backgrounds, consistent lighting across the set
- No watermarks, no visible third-party trademarks
- Update `imageAlt` in `data/products.js` to describe what the new image actually shows
- Lazy loading and responsive sizing are already handled in the markup
