Privacy policy

How information submitted through this website is handled.

Legal review required before launch. This policy is a working draft written to describe how this website actually behaves. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and it does not claim compliance with any specific privacy regulation. Have it reviewed against the laws applying to your company and your visitors before commercial launch.

What this policy covers

This policy covers information submitted through the forms on this website and information collected automatically by the hosting provider. It applies to this website only.

Information collected

When you submit a form, the fields you complete are collected. Across the quote, contact, manufacturing and custom sourcing forms these may include your name, company name, business email, country, phone number, product requirement, quantity, specification, destination, timeline and any notes or files you attach.

Your browser also stores your enquiry list locally on your own device so it survives between pages. That list stays in your browser and is not transmitted anywhere until you submit a form.

How information is used

Submitted details are used to respond to your enquiry, to source or quote the products you asked about, and to maintain a record of the correspondence. They are not published on this website, not sold, and not used for unrelated marketing.

Sharing with suppliers

To source a product, the technical requirement may need to be shared with potential suppliers. Where that happens, only the information needed to obtain a quotation is shared.

Retention

Enquiry records are retained for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting business relationship, and for any period required by applicable law.

Your requests

You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing the company. Requests are handled subject to any legal obligation to retain records.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies and includes no analytics script. Browser local storage is used only for the enquiry list described above. If analytics is added later, this section must be updated before it goes live.

Third-party services

Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to that service. If a form service is configured by the site owner, submissions pass through that provider. Both are subject to their own privacy terms.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect submitted information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and no absolute guarantee is given.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always the one published on this page.

Contact
